## What changed
In `compose.yml`, NATS now reads `deploy/nats/nats.conf` (`max_payload`
8 MiB). A new one-shot `nats-init` service creates the four JetStream streams
of `proto/nats/streams/` before api, propagator, ros2-publisher and store
start (`condition: service_completed_successfully`).

## What the others relied on (before → after)
- NATS `max_payload`: 1 MiB (default) → 8388608 bytes (checked in `/varz`;
  a 7 MiB publish to `trajectories.computed` is stored in `TRAJECTORIES`).
- Streams in compose: none → `LAUNCHES`, `TRAJECTORIES`, `ERRORS`, `CONFIG`
  exist when api, propagator, ros2-publisher and store start. The services
  should not create or edit streams. They still create their own durable
  consumers (`proto/README.md` > Streams).
- Start order: those four services waited for `nats` healthy → they wait for
  `nats-init` to exit 0. If NATS is not reachable, `nats-init` exits 1 and
  they do not start.
- A stream that already exists is left unchanged. A change to a file in
  `proto/nats/streams/` reaches a running NATS only after the NATS container is
  recreated (JetStream data is on tmpfs) or after a manual
  `nats stream edit`.
- Same environment variables and ports (`NATS_URL=nats://nats:4222`, 4222,
  8222).
- viz-web and control-web do not depend on `nats-init` yet: their compose
  services have no `NATS_URL` today. Add the dependency when their servers
  connect to NATS.
